At Abimbola Kumapayi Chambers, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy and protecting your personal data. Our goal is to ensure transparency, trust, and full compliance with applicable data protection laws.

3. Use of Cookies

Our website uses minimal, essential cookies necessary for performance and functionality. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Certain third-party tools, such as our ConvertKit subscription forms, may use their own cookies for functionality. These are managed directly by their providers.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features of the site may not function properly without them.

We use ConvertKit to manage our email subscriptions and deliver resources such as eBooks, guides, and newsletters. ConvertKit may collect limited technical data (e.g., your IP address or device type) to ensure proper form performance and delivery.

For more information on ConvertKit’s privacy practices, please review their policy at https://convertkit.com/privacy.

5. Contact Forms and Communication

Our website’s contact form uses a secure PHP backend to process and send inquiries directly to our team. The information submitted is stored securely and never shared with third parties unless legally required or expressly authorized by you.
