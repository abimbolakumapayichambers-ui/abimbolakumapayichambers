Civil law governs the private rights and obligations that exist between individuals and organizations. Our firm handles a broad range of civil matters, including contract enforcement, landlord–tenant issues, tort claims, and compensation disputes.

We use negotiation, mediation, and litigation strategies to achieve resolutions that protect your interests while preserving valuable relationships whenever possible.

Immigration Law

Immigration law governs who may enter, stay, or work in a country — and under what conditions. Our team assists individuals, families, and corporations with visa applications, work permits, residency, asylum, and citizenship. Our team has extensive experience assisting clients who have faced visa denials or complex immigration challenges—helping them understand what went wrong, rebuild stronger applications, and achieve successful outcomes with integrity and diligence.

At Abimbola Kumapayi Chambers, we simplify the complex world of global mobility. Whether you’re relocating for work, family, or investment, our lawyers ensure that your transition is compliant, efficient, and stress-free.

We handle global immigration matters across Africa, the UK, the EU, and North America — bridging borders through lawful, personalized solutions.

Start Your Immigration Journey

Emigration Law

Emigration law focuses on helping individuals and families lawfully exit their country of residence — whether permanently or temporarily — while maintaining compliance with local and international legal obligations.

We provide assistance in relocation planning, asset transfers, tax clearance, dual citizenship, and foreign investment migration programs. Our experienced counsel ensures you transition smoothly without legal complications.

Whether you’re moving abroad for business, education, or retirement, we’ll help you navigate every legal detail to safeguard your future.

Book an Emigration Consultation

Wills & Probate Law

A Will ensures your loved ones are cared for and your assets distributed according to your wishes. Probate, on the other hand, gives your appointed executors legal authority to manage and distribute your estate after your passing.

At Abimbola Kumapayi Chambers, we help clients draft valid Wills, navigate probate applications, and resolve estate disputes — both in Nigeria and internationally. Our estate planning services cover multiple jurisdictions to ensure seamless administration wherever your assets are located.

Preparing your Will today means peace of mind for tomorrow. Let us help you protect your legacy and your family’s future.
